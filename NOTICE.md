## Minecraft item icons

The pictures in `public/items/` show Minecraft items inside the inventory window. They are
artwork owned by Mojang AB and are included only to label items in this free tool.
They are not covered by Beacon's GPL-3.0 license (or by any license from this project),
and no rights to them are granted. They are shipped next to the program as separate files.

Where they come from:

- **Item and block textures** (about 1050 icons): from the Minecraft 26.1 resource pack, via the
  [PrismarineJS/minecraft-assets](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-assets) repository.
- **Inventory renders** (about 450 icons, for items the game draws from a 3D model, such as the
  shield, beds, banners, stairs and slabs): from the
  [Minecraft Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki), licensed
  [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/).
  Some have been reduced to a single frame to save space.
- **Item names, stack sizes** (`src/lib/minecraft-item-list.json`): generated from
  [PrismarineJS/minecraft-data](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data) (MIT).

If you are a rights holder and want something removed, open an issue and it will be taken out.
